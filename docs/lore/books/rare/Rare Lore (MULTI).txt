Title: ZC Labs Made Zombkolaps Potion!


Author: Discoveries Against Zombies (DAZ) Foundation


Page One: We received a report from an anonymous whistleblower within ZC Labs Inc that the company developed a potion that spreads Zombkolaps! The DAZ Foundation believes that the report is credible; our watchers noted a decrease of ZC Lab CCC agent activity while ZC Labs’ laboratories remain functional. It’s implausible that the labs can maintain their experiments and near 100% cure rate without surplus zombies.


Page Two: The DAZ Foundation wholeheartedly condemns the development of a Zombkolaps potion. For the sake of everyone, we demand that ZC Labs provide a truthful statement about this development that commits to the destruction of all copies of the Zombkolaps potion and its associated recipe.


Page Three: This potion threatens everyone. If released or sold, this potion can be used to assassinate anyone or wipe out villages. This could be used as a weapon for warfare, where these weapons can be used to overwhelm entire areas of the world by outpacing cure production. And in a world where Zombkolaps ceases to exist, this potion could start the Kolaps all over again.


Page Four: ZC Labs must commit to destroying this potion for all of our sakes.


Page Five: A world without zombies is possible. We just have to fight for it.


Title: ZC Labs Created The Zombkolaps!


Author: Conspiracy Uncovered


Page One: ZC Labs’s lies have been revealed again! They made the Zombkolaps and cannot hide it anymore! Everyone pretends that ZC Labs just invented the Zombkolaps potion, but we all know that they can’t hide that they can manufacture it in their labs! So they turn their lies into a marketable poison for Governors to control us more easily!


Page Two: ZC Labs’s betrayal of the world is more visible than ever. They released this virus to destroy our society. Keep your eyes out, being able to think critically is now criminalized by the system!


Page Three: It is no coincidence that these labs spread with the Confederation. Governors seek to use the Zombkolaps as a way to profit from everyday citizens, both directly through the insane fees needed to access the “cure” from the banks and through making enemies of the state disappear. Now, Governors don’t even need a zombie to make you disappear, they bottle it up and can throw it at you at a moment’s notice.


Page Four: It’s all a conspiracy for the governors, the labs, and the banks to control everything. We must resist, we cannot let these groups rule the world!
