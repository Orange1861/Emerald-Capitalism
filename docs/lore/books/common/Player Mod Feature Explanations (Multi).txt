Title: What are Emerald Golems?


Author: Sensu OrangeVillager32


Page One: Above all else, the emerald golem represents the power of the banks. 


Page Two: The quick and fast hitting emerald golem serves as a key defense to any village. While not as sturdy as its more simplistic cousin, the mighty iron golem, banks can rapidly manufacture them to fight off any threats to a village. 


Page Tree: They’re built similar to an iron golem, but take four emerald blocks instead of four iron blocks.


Page Four: Meanwhile, compared to their smaller but more versatile emerald skirmishers, they are a true war machine and protector of villages. Don’t expect them to clean up the mess though.
